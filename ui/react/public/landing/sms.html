Text messages from Avi Household Organizer

Avi Household Organizer is a private service run by Benjamin Maisano (sole proprietor) for the members of his household. It keeps the family's calendar, reminders, documents and records, and household members can text it to add or look something up.

How to opt in: a household member whose mobile number has been added to their profile by the household admin texts SUBSCRIBE (or JOIN) to (973) 370-9121. Avi replies with the program details below and asks them to reply Y to confirm. Once they reply Y, Avi confirms the subscription and from then on answers their texts and sends the reminders they ask for. By texting the keyword and replying Y you agree to the Terms and Privacy Policy.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help and STOP to opt out at any time. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Numbers that are not registered with the household receive no messages beyond the one-time reply to SUBSCRIBE, JOIN or HELP.

What you'll receive

  • Replies to messages you send to Avi.
  • Reminders you asked for, such as an appointment or an ID that is about to expire.
  • Confirmations when Avi files something you sent, such as a document photo.

No marketing or promotional messages are ever sent.
